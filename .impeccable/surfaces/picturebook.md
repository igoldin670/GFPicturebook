## Direction contract

THESIS: A handbound photo journal, with photographs visible immediately rather than an oversized slogan and upload dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue bookcloth (#203B50), warm paper (#F4EFE5), Bodoni Moda roman/italic lettering, Inter controls, restrained copper, tactile white Polaroids. No stock marketing illustration or generic metric cards.

STORY: Sign into a personal album, browse photos/videos, open a dated chapter, and organize missing dates without leaving the collection.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A small orbit wordmark above an ink-blue left rail; centered two-line title, shallow centered upload surface, compact collection/filter/selection toolbar, and complete Polaroids visible within a 900px desktop viewport. Phone navigation is a compact horizontal cloth strip.
